    chevron_right help How does resolution work?

    How to get the best resolution

    • The output is only as sharp as your smallest source image. Enlarging a small photo cannot invent detail — it just gets blurry.
    • Animations and videos center every transformed frame on one canvas, sized to the widest and tallest frame. Smaller frames get padding, without another resize or crop.
    • Leave Width/Height empty to keep the original image sizes inside that canvas. TIFF/PDF pages keep their individual sizes.
    • Fit uses Width/Height as shrink-only bounds. Exact — pad keeps the whole image in a chosen canvas; crop fills it by removing edges. Exact modes may enlarge images.
    • Tagged SDR colors are converted to sRGB before metadata is removed. Animation frames use 8-bit samples; GIF has binary transparency and Video decoding is opaque; use APNG for transparent editing round trips. Animation padding is transparent unless you choose a background. MP4 is opaque, defaults to black, and adds a right/bottom pixel when needed for even dimensions.
    • For pixel-art or screenshots, prefer PNG/APNG (lossless). For photos, WebP gives the best size-to-quality ratio.
